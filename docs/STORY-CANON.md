# THE CRITIC: COMING ATTRACTIONS — story canon

Duke Phillips demands a glowing review to rescue his television ratings. Jay Sherman refuses. Duke has kidnapped Marty Sherman and activates an irresponsible experimental broadcast system. The Coming Attractions become real and pour out of television screens into New York. Jay pursues Duke to rescue his son and stop the broadcast. Franklin’s replay route makes him an ally in that pursuit; Jay remains Marty’s father.

The opening takes place on the Coming Attractions review set. Jay is seated. Duke approaches him, and they share the same space through the initial exchange. Marty is concealed until Duke’s motivation line. These approved lines remain verbatim:

> DUKE: “Ratings are low. I need you to give this a glowing review, Sherman!”
>
> JAY: “It Stinks!”
>
> DUKE: “I thought you might say that... Allow me to give you a little motivation...”
>
> MARTY: “Dad!”
>
> JAY: “Marty!”
>
> DUKE: “If television can't bring the audience to us, perhaps we'll just bring the television to the audience!”
>
> JAY: “Hatchi Matchi!!!”

Monitors show recognizable members of the actual game cast. They surge and release several creatures in succession. A Shermometer strikes Jay and launches him out of the studio. The opening ends before the street landing: the selected playable character drops into the actual Broadway gameplay canvas and uses the existing gravity, fall and landing poses. Skipping begins grounded at the same opening checkpoint. Franklin appears alongside Jay on Franklin’s route and follows the pursuit into gameplay.

Between major districts, Duke transports Marty in the same portable cage. The selected playable character follows. These short scenes give the chase a visible destination without interrupting ordinary fights. Dialogue resolves speaker, portrait, line and reaction for the selected route.

The established first four stages remain Broadway, Last Train Uptown, Above the Avenue and Theater District. Brown-haired Franklin remains the fourth-stage boss on Jay’s route, appearing as Baby New Year 1937. He unlocks when Jay defeats him and exits that Stage 4 attempt without dying. Franklin’s route uses Shermometer v3 there, so Franklin never fights himself.

The Palace projectionist first appears through choreographed eyes in shadow and then in a lit booth. Projection booths span the entire rear wall. She uses only powered booths currently visible to the player; the three circuit colors link groups of booths to three targets on the floor. Disabling a circuit shuts off its group, and disabling all three ends her attacks. Her film reels bounce through the aisle.

The supplied cream-scarf male figure is now the Palace Cinema boss. He is foreshadowed on the film screen and emerges from it into the fight. His approved display name is Violent Austrian Rabbi; the legacy `pizzeria-boss` ID is retained for compatibility. The service exit requires both his defeat and the complete projection-circuit shutdown. Spike, an African American character from the supplied new performances, blocks the pursuit outside the Little Italy pizzeria and attacks with trash cans.

The final tower shows Duke at the controls and Marty still confined. The active system continues transmitting Coming Attractions, sending repeated enemy waves from its screens during the machine fight. The final encounter has two required combat phases: disable the transmitter, then defeat Duke personally. Machine failure stops the transmissions and dismisses its remaining summons before Duke confronts the player. Duke fights as a furious executive, with faster pursuit, grounded physical attacks and punishable recovery. He has no monster transformation.

Only Duke’s defeat permits the rescue. The ending reunites Jay and Marty, gives the selected player a visible reaction or celebration, and returns to the existing results presentation. Franklin’s replay acknowledges his assistance without attributing Jay’s father-specific dialogue to him or announcing an existing unlock as new.
